Mathematician of the multitude. First Minister. Founder of the Plan.

Prime Radiant · projection stable

In the last centuries of Empire, when Trantor still mistook its own weight for immortality, one Heliconian mapped the fall. Psychohistory does not forecast a man; it forecasts the mob, the market, and the millennium. From the barren edge-world of Terminus the Foundation was seeded — not to halt thirty thousand years of darkness, but to cut them to a thousand.

  1. Large deviations require N galactic. Then λ2 is simple, I scales as N, and a single emperor is o(1).
  2. The ensemble does not observe ρ. Conditioning is the h-transform of Doob; it replaces V by V − 2D log h and the gap of the Plan is no longer the gap.
  3. V is autonomous across the Interregnum: human nature is the drift, not a parameter. Saints are not required; symmetry of ℒ on L2(ρ∞) is.
  4. The Vault opens iff λ2(t) ≤ λ*. That is a clock on the Poincaré constant, not a throne.
